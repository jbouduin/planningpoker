# Cookie guideline

## Cookie usage
My website is fully functional without the use of cookies.<br><br>
As long as the display language is German, no cookies are required. If you switch the display language to English, you have the choice to save this preference as a cookie. In case you decline, the display language will switch back to German with your next visit or when refreshing the browser content.
<br>

## The cookie optionally saved

- __Name:__ "language"
- __Purpose:__ Save the prefered display language
- __Expiry:__ 1 Year
