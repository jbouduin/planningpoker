# Privacy policy

The use of my website is generally possible without providing personal information. As far as possible, the collection of any personal data (such as name, address or e-mail address), happens on a voluntary basis. Without your express consent, this data will not be passed on to third parties.

I point out that data transmission over the Internet (e.g. communication by e-mail) exposes this data to security vulnerabilities. A complete protection against unauthorized access by third parties is not possible.

German law requires me to publish my contact details. The use of this data by third parties for the transmission of unsolicited advertising and information materials is strictly prohibited. I reserve the right to take legal action in case of unsolicited advertising, such as spam e-mails.
